Velmora Systems - Travel and Expense Policy
Document ID: FIN-POL-011
Version: 4.2
Effective date: 2025-02-01
Status: Current

1. Meals
The daily meal allowance is 60 USD for domestic travel and 85 USD for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

2. Receipts
A receipt is required for any single expense above 25 USD. Expenses must be submitted on Form EX-210 within 30 days of being incurred.

3. Approval limits
Line managers may approve expenses up to 1,000 USD. Directors may approve expenses up to 10,000 USD. Expenses above 10,000 USD require approval from the Vice President of Finance.

4. Air travel
Employees must book economy class for flights shorter than 6 hours. Business class may be booked for flights of 6 hours or longer with director approval.
