Velmora Systems - AeroLink X200 Product FAQ
Document ID: PRD-FAQ-009
Version: 2.3
Effective date: 2025-04-01
Status: Current

1. Specifications
The AeroLink X200 (model number AX-200-B) weighs 1.2 kg and has a battery life of up to 14 hours on a single charge.

2. Warranty
The AeroLink X200 is covered by a 24-month limited warranty from the date of purchase. The warranty does not cover water damage.

3. Charging
The device charges fully in 90 minutes using the supplied 65 W USB-C adapter.
