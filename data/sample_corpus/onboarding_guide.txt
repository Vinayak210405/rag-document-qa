Velmora Systems - New Employee Onboarding Guide
Document ID: HR-ONB-001
Version: 2.0
Effective date: 2025-01-01
Status: Current

1. First week
New employees receive a laptop on their first day. The laptop must be encrypted according to the IT Security Handbook before it is connected to the corporate network.

2. Probation
The probation period lasts 90 days. During probation, annual leave may be taken only with written approval from the line manager.

3. Buddy programme
Each new employee is assigned a buddy for the first 60 days. The buddy answers day-to-day questions but does not approve expenses or leave.
