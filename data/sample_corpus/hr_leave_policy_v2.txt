Velmora Systems - Leave Policy
Document ID: HR-POL-004
Version: 2.0
Effective date: 2025-01-01
Status: Current (supersedes version 1.0)

1. Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly and may be taken after the first month of employment.

2. Carry-over
Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days into the following year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March.

3. Parental leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers are entitled to 6 weeks of paid parental leave.

4. Sick leave
An employee who is absent for more than 3 consecutive working days must provide a doctor's certificate. Sick absences must be reported to the line manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence.
